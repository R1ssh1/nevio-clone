Pipes & Tubes



Champak Steel and Engg. Co. is a leading manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of a wide range of Pipes & Tubes in Mumbai, India. Our Pipes & Tubes are crafted using high-quality raw materials that undergo rigorous testing and quality control measures by our experts. All our products adhere to both National and International Industrial Standards.

We offer a diverse range of Pipes & Tubes, including Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Duplex Steel, Super Duplex Steel, Alloy Steel, Nickel Alloy, Titanium Alloy, Aluminium Alloy, and Cupro-Nickel Alloy. With the expertise of our skilled professionals, we are committed to delivering superior quality Pipes & Tubes known for their exceptional durability and reliability. Our products cater to various industries and are manufactured using the finest materials and advanced techniques, ensuring compliance with industry standards.
