Fasteners



Champak Steel and Engg. Co. is a leading manufacturer, exporter, and supplier of a wide range of fasteners in Mumbai, India. Our fasteners are crafted using high-quality raw materials, meticulously controlled, and tested by our experts to meet national and international industrial standards.

We offer fasteners in various types and grades, including Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Duplex Steel, Super Duplex Steel, Alloy Steel, Nickel Alloy, Titanium Alloy, and Cupro-Nickel Alloy. With the expertise of our skilled professionals, we provide superior-quality fasteners known for their exceptional strength and durability.

Our fasteners find applications across various industries, and our adept engineers utilize the finest materials and advanced techniques to manufacture products that meet industry standards. At Champak Steel and Engg. Co., we are committed to delivering reliable and high-quality fastening solutions to our customers.
